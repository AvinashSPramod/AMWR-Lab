Facilities & Instrumentation

Prototyping infrastructure, instrumented anatomical phantoms, and human-motion biomechanics equipment.

Experimental & Prototyping Infrastructure

Optical Motion Capture & Kinematics

Multi-camera optical motion capture setup for 3D tracking of anatomical landmarks, joint misalignment, and exoskeleton linkage trajectories during dynamic tasks.

Muscle Activity & Force Instrumentation

Wireless surface electromyography (sEMG) sensors, multi-axis load cells, inline cable tension sensors, and ground reaction force plates for quantifying physical assistance.

Instrumented Phantom Testbeds

Custom multi-degree-of-freedom shoulder and limb phantom benches for repeatable mechanical benchtop validation of joint kinematics, capstan friction, and parasitic loads prior to human trials.

Rapid Prototyping & Fabrication

3D CAD/CAE design workstations, multi-material 3D printing for early kinematic proof-of-concept prototypes, and precision CNC/machining access for load-bearing metal components.
