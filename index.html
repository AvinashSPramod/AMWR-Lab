Interdisciplinary Research Lab

INSPIRELab at IIT Madras

The INSPIRE Lab at IIT Madras focuses on development of safe and affordable robotic systems for surgical and image-guided interventions and their clinical translation.

The lab is led by Dr. Nirav Patel, Associate Professor in the Department of Engineering Design, and is an interdisciplinary space for mechanical, biomedical, computer science, and electrical engineering research.
