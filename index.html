About the Laboratory

Led by Dr. Avinash Seena Pramod in the Department of Industrial Design at the National Institute of Technology (NIT) Rourkela, the Advanced Mechanisms and Wearable Robotics (AMWR) Lab focuses on first-principles mechanical synthesis and hardware validation for physical human-robot interaction. Our research spans multi-degree-of-freedom upper-limb mechanisms, passive industrial lower-back exoskeletons, and 3D geodesic cable-driven soft exosuits.

Core Research Pillars

Mechanism Synthesis & Kinematics

Closed-loop linkage optimization, self-aligning anatomical joints, and passive spring-cam-pulley transmissions engineered for lightweight, unpowered assistance.

Industrial & Assistive Exoskeletons

Upper-limb and lower-back wearable systems designed to offload musculoskeletal strain during overhead tasks and repetitive industrial lifting.

Soft Cable-Driven Exosuits

3D geodesic cable-routing models and active anchor-point reconfiguration validated on instrumented phantom benches and human subjects.
