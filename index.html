About the Laboratory

Led by Dr. Avinash Seena Pramod in the Department of Industrial Design at the National Institute of Technology (NIT) Rourkela, the Advanced Mechanisms and Wearable Robotics (AMWR) Lab bridges fundamental mechanism synthesis with physical human-robot interaction. Our work emphasizes clean mechanical architecture—such as passive gravity-compensation linkages, tunable torque-phase mechanisms, and reconfigurable cable routing—validated through precision phantom testbeds and human-subject biomechanics trials.

Core Research Pillars

1. Mechanism Synthesis & Kinematics

Closed-loop linkage optimization, self-aligning joints, and passive spring-cam-pulley transmissions tailored to match anatomical joint kinematics without bulky actuation.

2. Industrial & Assistive Exoskeletons

Upper-limb and lower-back wearable devices engineered to offload musculoskeletal loads during repetitive overhead lifting and stoop-lifting tasks.

3. Soft Cable-Driven Exosuits

3D geodesic cable-path modeling, active cable-anchor reconfiguration, and textile-integrated force transmission validated on instrumented phantom benches and human subjects.
