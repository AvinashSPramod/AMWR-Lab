Ph.D. Positions (Full-Time / Sponsored)

Open to candidates with a Master's degree (or exceptional Bachelor's degree) in Mechanical Engineering, Industrial Design, Robotics, Biomedical Engineering, or allied disciplines through NIT Rourkela's regular Ph.D. admission cycles or externally funded fellowships (PMRF, CSIR/UGC, etc.).

Desired Skills: Strong mathematical and mechanical engineering fundamentals, understanding of mechanism design and control systems, proficiency in solid modelling, and familiarity with MATLAB, SolidWorks, Adams, ANSYS, and Python.
