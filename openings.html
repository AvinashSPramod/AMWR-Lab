Openings & Contact

Opportunities for Ph.D. scholars, project staff, M.Tech/B.Tech students, and research interns at NIT Rourkela.

We Are Recruiting!

The Advanced Mechanism and Wearable Robotics Lab at NIT Rourkela is looking for self-motivated researchers passionate about mechanical design, kinematics, and building physical robotic hardware.

Available Positions

Ph.D. Positions (Full-Time / Sponsored)

Open to candidates with a Master's degree (or exceptional Bachelor's degree) in Mechanical Engineering, Industrial Design, Robotics, Biomedical Engineering, or allied disciplines through NIT Rourkela's regular Ph.D. admission cycles or externally funded fellowships (PMRF, CSIR/UGC, etc.).

Desired Skills: Mechanism kinematics, 3D CAD (SolidWorks/Fusion 360), MATLAB, hardware prototyping, and experimental testing.

Project Staff (JRF / SRF / Project Associate)

Project positions on sponsored wearable robotics and exoskeleton development grants will be posted here as funded projects commence.

Status: [EDIT: Currently accepting expressions of interest / Check official NIT Rourkela recruitment portal]

NIT Rourkela M.Tech & B.Tech Students

Current students in the Department of Industrial Design (and allied departments) interested in thesis projects or semester design projects on exoskeletons, soft robotics, or testbed instrumentation are welcome to stop by the lab.

Summer / Winter Research Interns

Highly motivated external undergraduate/postgraduate students with hands-on CAD, 3D printing, or embedded instrumentation experience may apply for on-site internships (minimum 8 weeks).
