Dr. Avinash Seena Pramod

Assistant Professor, Department of Industrial Design, NIT Rourkela

Dr. Avinash S Pramod leads the Advanced Mechanism and Wearable Robotics Lab at NIT Rourkela. Prior to joining NIT Rourkela, he served as a faculty member at the Rajiv Gandhi Institute of Petroleum Technology (RGIPT) and completed his postdoctoral research at the Assistive and Rehabilitation Robotics Lab, Chung-Ang University (and HUrotics Inc.), Seoul, South Korea. He earned his Ph.D. from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Madras, focusing on wearable shoulder mechanisms and multi-degree-of-freedom shoulder phantom testbeds.
