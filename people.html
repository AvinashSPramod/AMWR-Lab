Principal Investigator

Dr. Avinash S Pramod

Assistant Professor, Department of Industrial Design, NIT Rourkela

Dr. Avinash S Pramod leads the Advanced Mechanism and Wearable Robotics Lab at NIT Rourkela. Prior to joining NIT Rourkela, he served as an Assistant Professor at the Rajiv Gandhi Institute of Petroleum Technology (RGIPT) and worked as a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Assistive and Rehabilitation Robotics Lab, Chung-Ang University, South Korea. He also served as a Technical Advisor to Hurotics Inc., a Seoul-based wearable robotics company. He received his M.S. and Ph.D. in Robotics from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Madras.
